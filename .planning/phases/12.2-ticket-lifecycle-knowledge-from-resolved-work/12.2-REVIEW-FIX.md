### Not done, and why

- **b-IN-01 (publish dialog conflict copy): not done.** The fix needs a dialog-specific Conflict row without the last sentence ("Your edits are still in the form; copy them first if you need them."). The UI-SPEC has no such row and no amendment adds one, and this batch may not invent copy. A possible amendment row, for the orchestrator or a UI checker to decide: "Conflict (dialog)" = "Nothing was changed. Someone else changed this proposal after you opened it. Reload it to see their version." (the existing row minus its last sentence), used by `PUBLISH_REFUSAL_ROWS.CONFLICT` and `REJECT_REFUSAL_ROWS.CONFLICT`.
- **b-WR-06, the success line:** UI-SPEC 3i asks for a `role="status"` line after a successful restart. A queued restart (`queued: true`) still has no text for it, because no copy row exists (the reviewer suggested asking for one, for example "Generation restarted."). The refresh and the lock are done. The A3 line for `queued: false` is unchanged.
- **b-IN-03, wording:** the count is now separated in the accessible name. A name that says the number counts drafts awaiting review (for example "Proposals, 3 drafts") would need a copy row, so it was not added.
- **b-WR-02, refresh failure:** if `router.refresh()` never delivers the saved version's props, Preview and "Approve and publish…" stay waiting with no reason line until a browser reload. No copy row exists for a reason here. The fields stay editable and Save works.
- **b-WR-03, closing after an unknown outcome:** the publish and reject dialogs reload the proposal (focus on the h1) when closed after an unknown outcome. This replaces UI-SPEC 3i's "closed without acting → the opener", because the opener may be gone once a committed publish or reject is shown. Recorded as a decision of this batch.
