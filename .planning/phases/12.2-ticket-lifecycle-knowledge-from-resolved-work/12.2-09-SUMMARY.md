# Phase 12.2 Plan 09: Review page, Sources card, status views and the publish and reject dialogs Summary

The review page `/kb/proposals/[id]` now exists. The server page calls `loadProposalReview` with the session actor before it renders anything. NOT_ALLOWED, MEMBERSHIP_ENDED and NOT_FOUND each get an EmptyState; another organization's id gives "Proposal not found". The DRAFT view is the editor, with client field errors from `validateDraftFields` through `fieldRefusalCopy`, a Preview of the server-rendered stored body, and an action bar in which "Approve and publish…" stays disabled while the form is dirty. The other statuses have their own views: GENERATING polls and offers restart when stuck, FAILED and REJECTED offer "Start a new proposal" through `StartProposalDialog`, and PUBLISHED offers "Retry indexing" while the embedding is not COMPLETED. The Sources card shows each source with its change warning and its citations. Publish and reject each need a confirmation dialog. Every outcome on the page and in both dialogs goes through `resolveActionOutcome`.

## How the plan's rules are met

- **Save (3c, 3h, A2, A4):** the client check runs first. A title marker or a title ticket link shows TITLE_CITATION_MARKER_ERROR under Title. A body marker shows CITATION_MARKER_ERROR under Body, and a body ticket link shows TICKET_LINK_ERROR under Body. Each moves focus to its field; for a Body error the Preview is switched off first. On success the component shows `toast.success`, resets the form, calls router.refresh() and focuses the h1. A field refusal from the server is handled like the client check. CONFLICT, the other refusals, a rejected call and UNKNOWN render the role=alert callout, which takes focus (tabIndex -1). Save stays disabled until reload whenever the resolver says so. The local edits stay in the fields, and "Reload proposal" appears only for CONFLICT.
- **Restart (3e, 3i, A3, C5):** while the proposal is GENERATING, the view polls every PROPOSAL_POLL_INTERVAL_MS. When `stuck` is true it shows the stuck notice with "Restart generation". A refusal, a rejected call or UNKNOWN puts a role=alert line under the button, which keeps focus; after an unknown outcome the button stays aria-disabled. A restart with `queued: false` keeps the A3 row in a role=status line outside the notice, until the proposal leaves GENERATING. A restart with `queued: true` adds no line. If a poll refresh removes the pressed button and focus went with it, focus moves to the line or, if there is no line, to the h1.
- **Retry indexing (A1, A5, P5):** the button shows while the embedding is FAILED or PENDING; with PENDING the hint line sits above it. On an ok result the resolver's line ("Indexing restarted." or RETRY_RECORDED_NOT_STARTED) is stored in `retryOutcomeLine`, router.refresh() runs, and the role=status line takes focus at once. The line is rendered outside the button block, so it stays after the refresh, including when COMPLETED removes the button. A refusal or an unknown outcome keeps the alert line under the button, with its focus and lock rule, and triggers no refresh.
- **Publish (3f, A1, A4):** focus opens on the checkbox. "Publish article" stays disabled until the box is checked. While the call is pending, Esc and an outside click do not close the dialog. On success the dialog closes and shows `toast.success` when `indexingQueued` is true, or `toast.warning` with the A1 row when it is false; then it refreshes and focuses the h1. A refusal, a conflict (with "Reload proposal"), a rejected call or UNKNOWN shows its row in the alert, leaves confirm disabled and moves focus to "Keep reviewing". The dialog never retries on its own.
- **Reject (3g):** focus opens on "Keep proposal", and the confirm button is the destructive "Reject proposal". Outcomes follow the same rules as publish.
- **Focus on close:** a dialog closed without acting returns focus to the button that opened it (explicit onCloseAutoFocus). After success or reload, the h1 takes focus.

## Deviations from Plan

None that change scope. The choices the plan left open are recorded as decisions in the frontmatter above. Two of them go beyond the plan text:

1. **[Rule 2 - Correctness] Preview disabled while the body is dirty.** The plan's Preview shows the server-rendered stored body, and it must never render the raw body on the client. With unsaved body edits it would preview text other than what is in the editor, so the toggle waits for a save and gives its reason.
2. **[Rule 2 - Correctness] aria-disabled for Restart generation and Retry indexing.** UI-SPEC 3i asks that the pressed button keep focus and also stay disabled after an unknown outcome. A natively disabled button cannot hold focus, so these two buttons use aria-disabled with a click guard. Playwright's `toBeDisabled` honours aria-disabled.
