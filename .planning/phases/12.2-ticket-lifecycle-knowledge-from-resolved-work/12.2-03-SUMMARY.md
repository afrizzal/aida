# Phase 12.2 Plan 03: Bounded LLM calls, typed errors and a race-safe embed job Summary

Ollama calls are bounded at 30 s from the request to the end of the response body by one AbortController deadline linked into the client's fetch; every adapter failure is classified as CONFIGURATION, INVALID_OUTPUT or TRANSIENT from its class and numeric status alone; and the shared kb-embed-article job now does every write under a FOR UPDATE lock on the article row, skips a changed text or model, and writes FAILED only on a current PENDING article.

## Ollama deadline (review C4)

`completeOllama` runs `withDeadline(OLLAMA_TIMEOUT_MS, (fetch) => new Ollama({ host, fetch }).chat(...))`. The client's non-streamed path awaits `fetch`, then `response.json()` with no signal of its own (`browser.mjs` `post` and `processStreamableRequest`); the deadline covers both. Proven with fake `setTimeout`/`clearTimeout` only: no answer, and headers followed by a body that never enqueues (the real client reached the stubbed fetch at `/api/chat`, the stub recorded the body abort), are pending at 29,999 ms and reject with `LlmTimeoutError` at 30,000 ms with `vi.getTimerCount()` 0. A caller signal that aborts first aborts the request (AbortError, not a timeout). Parse failures report `ollama: structured output parse failed` without the content.

## Header comment of the job

Protected:
- Two jobs for one article never leave two sets of chunks: the article row lock serializes the swap (delete, inserts and COMPLETED run in one transaction under that lock).
- A job whose text is no longer the article's text never swaps and never writes a status (a newer job owns the article).
- A job whose vectors came from a model other than the one configured when it holds the article lock never swaps and never writes a status, so after a model change followed by re-embed-all an old-model job cannot overwrite the new vectors or write COMPLETED with the old model id.
- FAILED is written only on a PENDING article that still has the job's text and model (markFailedIfCurrent, the job's only FAILED writer), so a failing job never overturns COMPLETED.

Not protected:
- The Setting rows are not locked, so a model change that commits after the job's model check and before its commit still lets that swap store the old model's vectors, tagged with the old model id. Retrieval filters chunks by the configured model (src/lib/rag/retrieve.ts), so they are not used, and the job that re-embed-all sends after the change replaces them.
- A model change without re-embed-all leaves an article whose job skipped at its status (normally PENDING) until re-embed-all or Retry indexing sends a job under the new model.

The comment also states the lock order (article row, then its chunks), why no unique index on KbChunk is added, and that the manual KB path and re-embed-all gain the same protection.
