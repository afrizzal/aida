## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| browser → merge action | Ticket ids are untrusted; the dialog is a convenience, the server enforces every rule (D-07) |
| concurrent writers → ticket rows | Merges, replies and link writes race on the same rows |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-12-12 | Information disclosure | merging tickets of different contacts | high | mitigate | DIFFERENT_CONTACTS under the row locks (K33); refusal test proves nothing changed |
| T-12-13 | Elevation of privilege | merge after member removal or across organizations | high | mitigate | Membership in the transaction; organization predicate on the locks; tests |
| T-12-14 | Tampering | partial merge | high | mitigate | One transaction; rollback seam test |
| T-12-15 | Repudiation | merge without audit | medium | mitigate | MERGED_INTO, MERGED_FROM, STATUS_CHANGED, RELATION_REMOVED and RELATION_ADDED rows, ids only; 12-o1 stays open (D-11) |
| T-12-16 | Tampering | agent reply landing on a merged source | medium | mitigate | lockTicketForAgentWrite under the row lock; forced-order test |
| T-12-17 | Denial of service | lock cycle between a merge and a customer reply | medium | mitigate | One lock order: mergeTickets and resolveReplyTicket (12-04) both lock through lockTicketsInIdOrder (ascending id, one statement); the reply re-checks after locking and redirects inside its transaction; forced-order tests in 12-04 prove the reply is stored once, the merge is all-or-nothing and no 40P01 occurs |
