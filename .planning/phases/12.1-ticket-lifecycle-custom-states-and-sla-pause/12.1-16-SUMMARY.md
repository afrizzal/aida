## Product defects

1. **Filter-trigger focus after "Remove deleted status" (UI-SPEC 4c, plan 12.1-13).** Observed: after the click the notice disappears and the URL loses `state`, but `#inbox-status-filter-trigger` is not focused (Playwright: "inactive"). Likely cause: `missing-status-notice.tsx` focuses the trigger before `router.replace(...)` runs. The navigation to new search params then renders the list panel again, with a new trigger, so the focus is lost. The fix belongs in `src/app/(app)/tickets/missing-status-notice.tsx` (and probably `filter-chip-row.tsx`), outside this plan's files. The assertion stays in the spec, unweakened, as `test.fixme` with this reason. Remove the fixme once the product focuses the trigger after the navigation.
