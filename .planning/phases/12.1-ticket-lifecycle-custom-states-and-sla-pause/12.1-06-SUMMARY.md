## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug, test] Unhandled 57P01 from the fresh pool in the migrated-resume test**
- Found during: Task 2 first verify (18 passed, exit 1).
- Issue: `freshPrisma.$disconnect()` does not end a pg pool passed to PrismaPg, and `src/lib/db.ts` does not expose its pool; when the harness container stopped, the idle connections got `FATAL 57P01` as an unhandled error.
- Fix: the test builds the fresh graph's client on a pool it owns and installs it with `vi.doMock("@/lib/db")` after `vi.resetModules()`; afterAll disconnects, ends the pool, `vi.doUnmock`, restores DATABASE_URL and the global client, and resets modules. runTicketMutation and readLegacyStateIds are the real modules from the fresh import. No source file changed.
- Commit: 4a3d7ed

**2. [Test detail] Tracer ticket carries a first response**
- The tracer asserts "no breach at the moved resolutionDueAt minus one minute". The first-response deadline is hours earlier and would breach first, so the tracer sets `firstRespondedAt` directly before the pause; only the resolution timer is live, as the plan's case intends.

**3. [Test strength] Extra cases**
- An at-risk window case (exactly 20 percent flags, 1 ms earlier does not) in sla-pause, and a paused ticket inside the at-risk window in sla-flag-handler (the at-risk pass's gate).
- Two-ticket orders B and C use a bulk state change to Pending (A uses the priority change the plan names); see decisions.
