## What changed

- `tests/e2e/support/db.ts` exports `stateIdFor(key)` and the `LegacyStatusKey` type. `stateIdFor` reads `readLegacyStateIds(prisma, orgId)` once per worker. It throws when the key has no active migrated state.
- **Saved views:** stored filters are `{ version: 2, view, stateIds }`. URL expectations use sorted `state=<ids>`, and a raw or encoded comma both match. The new case goes to `/tickets?status=OPEN` and asserts all of these:
  - the final URL is exactly `/tickets?state=<Open id>`;
  - there is no `status` param;
  - the seeded Open ticket is listed and the seeded Pending ticket is not.
- **mutations, bulk, snooze, merge, feed:** DB assertions compare `stateId` with `stateIdFor(...)` instead of enum literals. The UI steps did not change: menuitemradio and option names are still state names.
- **command-palette:** the stale-result stub carries `stateName: "Open"` and `colorToken: "indigo"` (the migrated Open state's colour). Its saved view is version 2.
- **snooze:** closed tickets are seeded in the Closed state. Its legacy-view case now stores version 2 without the `snoozed` key and keeps its intent: a view without the field opens with snoozed off.
- **auto-close:** seeds are Resolved. A new assertion checks that ticket A ends in the Closed state; B stays Resolved.
- **public-status:** asserts that the public chip reads the group label `Resolved` before the follow-up.
