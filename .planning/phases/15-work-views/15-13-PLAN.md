## Decisions recorded by this plan

The outline fixes this plan's purpose and its files (`src/components/projects/list/*`, `list/actions.ts`, the bulk E2E); plan 15-12 fixes the leading slot, the toolbar start slot, the action file and the reload after success. Where they are silent, these choices were made inside 15-CONTEXT and are recorded here:

1. **Selection covers visible loaded rows only.** The visible rows are the loaded rows of expanded groups in display order (group order, then row order). The selection is a list of ids plus an anchor; after every change of the visible rows (groups reload, Load more, grouping, sort, filter, collapse) it is cut to the rows still visible. The ids a bulk call sends are always the selection intersected with the visible rows at the moment of the call, in display order, so a hidden or unloaded task is never sent even before the cut runs.
2. **Range.** A plain toggle sets the anchor to that row. A Shift toggle computes the clicked row's new state (the opposite of its current state) and applies it to every visible row between the anchor and the clicked row, both included; the anchor then moves to the clicked row. An anchor that is no longer visible makes the Shift toggle a plain toggle. Keyboard: Space toggles (the checkbox's own activation); Shift+Space is the range, recorded on keydown and read by the following click, because a keyboard-activated click does not carry the modifier in every browser. The UI-SPEC names Shift+click; Shift+Space is its keyboard equivalent.
3. **More than 100.** Selecting more than BULK_MAX rows is allowed (select all never cuts the list, so its label stays true); while more than 100 are selected every picker is aria-disabled with "Bulk too many reason" and nothing can be sent. `runBulkUpdate` also refuses locally above 100 or at 0 without calling the server, mirroring the service, so a refused request is never sent and never cut to 100.
4. **Select all loaded** is a two-state checkbox: checked exactly when every visible row is selected; activating it when checked clears the selection, otherwise selects every visible row. No mixed state, because the installed checkbox shows the same check mark for mixed, which would read as "all". It renders only while at least one row is visible (empty states and all-collapsed show none).
5. **Archived project.** Rows stay selectable (a read-only action) and the bar shows every picker aria-disabled with "Archived reason" (P12: disabled with a reason, never hidden; the UI-SPEC hides only quick add). Precedence of reasons: archived, then too many; pending and the lock after NOT_FOUND, MEMBERSHIP_ENDED or UNKNOWN disable without a reason line because the applying line or the callout explains them.
6. **Pickers.** Status, Assignee, Priority and Milestone are dropdown menus that apply on choosing an item. Start date and Due date are popovers with a native date input, "Apply to selected" and "Clear date" (applies null). Add labels and Remove labels are popovers with a checkbox list of the project's labels and "Apply to selected". Client checks: a valid CalendarDate before a date Apply; at least one label before a label Apply; for Add labels, no selected loaded task may end above MAX_TASK_LABELS (counted from each row's loaded labels) and at most 20 labels may be chosen, with the existing "Labels max reason". There is no client check of start against due per task: the service decides it for the whole batch (that is the all-or-none proof in the E2E).
7. **Success copy.** The toast uses the service's `changedCount` (tasks already holding the value are not counted), so the number is true.
8. **Selection survives a frame reload.** `ListSelectionProvider` wraps `ViewFrame` inside `ListView`, keyed by the same resetKey, so the frame's remount after UNKNOWN or a callout Reload keeps the selection (UI-SPEC: "selection kept"), then cut to the reloaded rows. The bar sits inside the frame, so its pending lock ends exactly when the reload remounts it.
9. **Outcomes.** Every result goes through `runBulkUpdate` and `resolveProjectOutcome(result, "bulkUpdateTasks", "bulk")`. Success: toast, clear, reload the groups like quick add, focus Select all loaded. Refusal: callout focused, selection kept, pickers usable unless disableUntilReload. UNKNOWN or rejected: warning notice, `reload()` at once, bar pending until the remount. No call is ever repeated by itself; a pending guard ignores a second apply.
10. **Action.** `bulkUpdateTasksAction(projectId, taskIds, patch)` in `list/actions.ts` (contract 18.1 row already lists bulkUpdateTasks; no operation is added): `projectWriteAction("bulkUpdateTasks", ...)`, ids through idOf, only the eight BulkTaskPatch keys copied from a plain object, revalidation `{ path: projectPath(projectId), type: "layout" }` as quick add. `list-view.tsx` imports the action directly (a client component may import a server-action module), so `list/page.tsx` (15-12) is not changed.
11. **Focus.** Row toggles keep focus on the checkbox; success and "Clear selection" move focus to the Select all loaded checkbox (the bar is gone), or to the project heading when no row remains after the reload; refusal and UNKNOWN focus the bar's callout.
12. **Gap rows** (deviation from outline convention 3, as 15-05, 15-09 to 15-12 did): `BULK_DATE_REQUIRED_REASON` = "Choose a date first.", `BULK_LABELS_REQUIRED_REASON` = "Choose at least one label first.", `BULK_LABELS_EMPTY` = "This project has no labels yet.", appended after the last gap row present at execution time with the next free G numbers, marked "written by plan 15-13" and pinned in `tests/unit/project-copy.test.ts`. Those two files are also appended to by plan 15-11 in wave 5; the sharing is allowed only because the orchestrator executes the wave's plans one at a time on one checkout and every edit is append-only.
13. **Extra files** (deviation): `tests/unit/project-list-selection.test.ts` (new, no other owner); `src/lib/projects/ui-copy.ts` and `tests/unit/project-copy.test.ts` (decision 12). Inside the outline's `list/*` the selection module is split into the pure `list-selection.ts` and the React `selection-controls.tsx`, and the pickers live in `bulk-pickers.tsx` beside `bulk-bar.tsx`.
