1. **Keyboard model (P3, C3; recorded deviation from D-12's wording).** The grid is one Tab stop with a roving tabindex. Outside editing: arrows move the active stop without wrapping; Home and End go to the first and last stop of the row; Ctrl+Home and Ctrl+End to the first stop of the first row and the last stop of the last row (the keys of the ARIA grid pattern the UI-SPEC names); Tab and Shift+Tab leave the grid natively.
