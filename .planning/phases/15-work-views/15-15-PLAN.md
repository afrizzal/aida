## Decisions recorded by this plan

The outline fixes this plan's purpose, its files (`[projectId]/table/page.tsx`, `loading.tsx`, `actions.ts`, `src/components/projects/table/*`, the spec), the scope-neutral export rule and that P3 and C3 are settled here. Plans 15-04, 15-05 and 15-12 fix the read shape, the outcome map and the frame. Where they are silent, these choices were made inside 15-CONTEXT and bind plan 15-20:

1. **Keyboard model (P3, C3; recorded deviation from D-12's wording).** The grid is one Tab stop with a roving tabindex. Outside editing: arrows move the active stop without wrapping; Home and End go to the first and last stop of the row; Ctrl+Home and Ctrl+End to the first stop of the first row and the last stop of the last row (the keys of the ARIA grid pattern the UI-SPEC names); Tab and Shift+Tab leave the grid natively. Enter or F2 starts editing an editable cell. In editing: Enter commits and focus stays on the cell; Escape cancels and restores; Tab and Shift+Tab commit and move to the next or previous editable cell, not editing. "The next editable cell in the row" continues on the next (or previous) task row when the row has none left, skipping group and footer rows; at the grid's last (or first) editable cell focus stays on the cell. D-12's "Tab moves to the next cell, focus stays in the grid" therefore holds in editing only, so keyboard users can leave the grid. The E2E proves both Tab paths.
2. **Stops.** A data cell's stop is its `td` (role gridcell, implicit in the grid table); the Key cell's stop is its `TaskLink`, so Enter on it opens the task the native way. A group header row has one stop (its toggle button); a footer row has up to two (Load more, Add task) and Left and Right move between them. Up and Down keep a preferred column across one-stop rows.
