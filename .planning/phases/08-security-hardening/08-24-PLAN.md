Table cells escape `|` as `\|`, and the parser splits only on unescaped `|`. In every quoted text, the home directory becomes `~`, and the value after `PASSWORD`, `SECRET`, `PEPPER` or `_KEY` followed by `=` or `:` becomes `<redacted>`. A quote is cut at its first line break and at 300 characters. ANSI escape sequences are stripped before any match.

## Docker health rule (review R2-2)

3. A `blocked (docker unhealthy)` row is never recorded as passed or failed.
