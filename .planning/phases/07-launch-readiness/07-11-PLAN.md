<!-- Product facts the docs must describe correctly. Do not paraphrase from memory. -->

**Site:** Astro Starlight in `website/`, `site: 'https://afrizzal.github.io'`, `base: '/aida'`. Internal links must be root-relative INCLUDING the base (`/aida/configuration/ai-providers/`) or written as Starlight-relative links — verify whichever form the scaffold's existing links use and be consistent. The scaffold currently has one sidebar group (`getting-started`) and two pages (`index.mdx`, `getting-started/quick-start.md`).

**AI providers** (`src/lib/llm/`): OpenAI, Anthropic and Ollama adapters behind one abstraction, selected in **Settings > AI Features**, with a Test Connection button. Provider API keys are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM (`src/lib/crypto/secret-box.ts`) into `Setting.value`, which requires `APP_ENCRYPTION_KEY` to be set. AI is off unless the `aiEnabled` toggle is on; with it off the helpdesk is fully functional.

**Embeddings** are configured separately from the chat provider (also in Settings > AI Features): OpenAI or Ollama, 768 dimensions, stored in pgvector. A "Re-embed all" action re-queues every KB article. Without an embedding provider, KB articles stay in `PENDING` embedding status and RAG retrieval returns nothing — which is exactly the demo dataset's honest state.

**Email channel** (Settings > Email): a per-workspace mailbox — IMAP host/port/TLS/user/password for inbound, SMTP host/port/TLS/user/password plus a from-address for outbound, a channel on/off toggle, Test Connection for both, and a health line driven by `email:lastPollAt` / `email:lastPollError`. Inbound polling is a recurring worker job; threading uses In-Reply-To/References with a `[#N]` subject-token fallback; a requester reply to a RESOLVED/CLOSED ticket auto-reopens it. Credentials are encrypted at rest. Outbound sends are queued jobs with a retry affordance and a Failed-to-send state.

**Branding** (Settings > Branding, from Plan 07-03): a single field — the workspace display name — applied to the app sidebar, the public request and status pages, and the outbound email from-name. Defaults to the organization name. Logo upload and a public tagline are both deferred — do not document either.

**Public portal:** `/request` (web intake form with a honeypot and per-IP rate limiting, attachments byte-sniffed and size/MIME-capped) and `/status/<token>` (tokenised public thread showing only PUBLIC messages, with follow-up replies that auto-reopen and a 1-5 CSAT rating once resolved).

**AIDA Insight** (`/insights`): 7/30/90-day periods; a background pg-boss job clusters recurring issues, detects KB gaps against the embedded knowledge base, computes volume drivers and an SLA/CSAT summary, and writes an AI narrative. Outputs cite the underlying tickets and are reproducible from stored params. Requires both a chat provider and an embedding provider for a live run.

**Demo mode** (Plan 07-07): `DEMO_MODE=true` on a fresh database, credentials in `.env.example`, seeded AI artifacts are stored data (`provider: "demo"`, `model: "demo-seed"`), never for internet-facing instances.

**Backup/restore** (Plan 07-04): `./scripts/backup.sh [DIR]` and `./scripts/restore.sh DB_DUMP UPLOADS_TARBALL [--yes]`. A complete backup is the Postgres dump AND the uploads volume. `docs/OPERATIONS.md` is the authoritative runbook.

**Security:** `docs/SECURITY.md` is the engineering security model (reconciled by Plan 07-09); `.github/SECURITY.md` is the disclosure policy. The site's security page summarises and links both — it does not fork them.

**Honest-claims rule (CLAUDE.md)** applies to every sentence: no invented metrics, never "trained"/"fine-tuned", relative claims only.
