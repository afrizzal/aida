## Why AIDA

Most helpdesk software treats AI as a paywalled add-on with per-resolution fees, while your
ticket data lives on someone else's servers. AIDA is different: AI is the *core*, not an
afterthought. Self-host it on your own server, point it at any LLM — including a fully local
one via Ollama — and keep every ticket on your own infrastructure.
